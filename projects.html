Smart Traffic Control

YOLO-Based Intelligent Traffic Signal Management System ↗

A traffic control system that uses YOLOv8 to detect and count vehicles across four lanes in real time, and adjusts signal timings according to traffic density to reduce congestion and waiting time.

  • Dynamic signals. Green time ranges from 5 to 20 seconds depending on lane density, with a 2-second yellow before each switch.
  • Vehicle classification. Cars, buses, trucks and motorcycles are identified from live video feeds.
  • Live dashboard. A Tkinter interface shows lane feeds, vehicle counts and congestion status.
  • Traffic analysis. Real-time Matplotlib graphs track density trends across the lanes.
  • Python
  • YOLOv8
  • OpenCV
  • NumPy
  • Tkinter
  • Matplotlib
  • Pillow
