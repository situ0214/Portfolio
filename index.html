Welcome to my little space on the internet.

I am an engineering graduate from Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering at GGV Bilaspur, with a keen interest in technology, problem-solving, and scientific exploration.

I am currently developing my expertise in Machine Learning (ML) and Deep Learning (DL), with a particular interest in their applications in healthcare. My curiosity about genomics and neuroscience has led me to explore how ML techniques applied to blood-based gene-expression data can help identify patterns associated with the early detection of neurodegenerative diseases. I believe that practical application is fundamental to understanding a concept, and I strive to put this into practice through my explorations.

With a keen interest in scientific research, I had the opportunity to undertake a summer research internship at the Department of Mathematics and Computing, IIT Patna, where I gained valuable exposure to the research process and academic environment. I am keen to build upon this experience by exploring new research directions, learning from diverse perspectives, and contributing to meaningful research.
